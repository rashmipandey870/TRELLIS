# TRELLIS 14 Signature Innovations: Phase Roadmap & Acceptance Test Suite

As mandated by Section 22 of the Master Prompt, these 14 signature innovations distinguish TRELLIS. Each innovation is tied to a specific delivery phase, concrete purpose, and rigorous acceptance test. None may feed an automated employment decision.

---

## Innovations Matrix

| # | Innovation Name | Delivery Phase | Core Purpose & Value | Concrete Acceptance Test | Feature Flag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Tamper-Evident Evidence Ledger** | Phase 1 (Foundation), Phase 2 (UI) | Append-only SHA-256 hash-chained log of all skill evidence using RFC 8785 canonical JSON; backed by off-chain crypto-shredding for DPDP compliance. | **Test**: Manually altering or tampering with a historical row in `evidence_ledger` is immediately detected during validation and raises a critical security audit event. | `FEATURE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER` |
| **2** | **"Why this?" & "What would change this?"** | Phase 2 | Full transparency on every skill recommendation, displaying confidence level, supporting evidence, and the exact new evidence needed to shift it. | **Test**: Automated UI test proves that no recommendation card renders in the interface without both explanatory panels present and populated. | `FEATURE_EXPLAINABLE_RECS` |
| **3** | **Consent Passport & Access Receipts** | Phase 1 | Centralized data sovereignty screen where employees manage active consents and inspect an immutable audit of who viewed their data. | **Test**: A line manager viewing an employee's consented Growth Ring summary produces an access receipt visible to the employee within 60 seconds. | Core Platform Feature |
| **4** | **Fair-Assessment Audit (IRT & DIF)** | Phase 2 | Employs 2-parameter Item Response Theory and Differential Item Functioning to detect culturally or demographically unfair test questions. Flagged questions go to human review. | **Test**: A synthetic dataset with a deliberately seeded biased question results in the item being automatically flagged for review without being auto-deleted. | `FEATURE_FAIR_ASSESSMENT` |
| **5** | **Knowledge-Risk Map** | Phase 4 | Aggregate enterprise heatmap identifying critical capabilities held by an dangerously low number of employees, with one-click mentoring/wiki capture workflows. | **Test**: A capability possessed by only 2 individuals displays only at the aggregate team level with names suppressed (`n < 5` rule enforced). | `FEATURE_KNOWLEDGE_RISK` |
| **6** | **First-90-Days Copilot** | Phase 1 (Core), Phase 2 (Pacing) | Replaces static induction checklists by orchestrating HR, IT, and learning tasks, assigning buddies, and measuring empirical *time-to-productivity*. | **Test**: The new hire sees exactly one prioritised next action per day, while the line manager sees strictly milestone progress without granular telemetry. | `FEATURE_FIRST_90_DAYS` |
| **7** | **Capability What-If Simulator** | Phase 4 | Simulates potential skill gains from learning interventions with uncertainty confidence intervals; provides honest feedback when history is insufficient. | **Test**: When executed on a skill with insufficient historical data, the simulator refuses to output an arbitrary number and renders "Not enough data". | `FEATURE_WHAT_IF_SIMULATOR` |
| **8** | **Learning-Impact Experiments** | Phase 4 | Evaluates training effectiveness against Kirkpatrick Levels 1–4 using matched cohort or before/after experimental designs. | **Test**: Every reported impact figure explicitly displays its statistical methodology, sample size ($n$), and stated limitations; never ungrounded claims. | `FEATURE_IMPACT_EXPERIMENTS` |
| **9** | **Teach-Back Verification** | Phase 3 | Enables learners to explain concepts in their own words via text/audio; AI evaluates against rubrics, and a human trainer/peer confirms and signs off. | **Test**: Teach-back evidence appended to the Growth Ring displays the verified signature and ID of the approving human reviewer. | `FEATURE_TEACH_BACK` |
| **10** | **Learning-Load Guard** | Phase 3 | Analyzes calendar free time, monitors weekly learning load, and warns managers before over-assigning training past healthy thresholds. | **Test**: Assigning coursework that exceeds the weekly threshold requires an explicit manager business reason and writes a warning audit entry. | `FEATURE_LOAD_GUARD` |
| **11** | **Skills Passport** | Phase 5 | Enables employees to export selected Growth Ring credentials as portable, tamper-proof W3C Verifiable Credentials / Open Badges 3.0 that they own. | **Test**: An exported credential file verifies successfully offline against public keys and exposes only the specific competencies selected by the employee. | `FEATURE_SKILLS_PASSPORT` |
| **12** | **Cognitive Accessibility Suite** | Phase 1 (Theme), Phase 2 (Tools) | Focus mode, chunked micro-lessons, reading ruler, calm colors, dyslexia fonts, and pausable timers designed for neurodivergent learners. | **Test**: 100% of learner pathways and assessment flows execute and pass automated a11y tests with all cognitive accessibility modes toggled on. | `FEATURE_COGNITIVE_A11Y` |
| **13** | **Privacy-by-Design Analytics** | Phase 4 | Enforces differential privacy, small-group cell suppression (`n < 5`), and a visible privacy budget on leadership dashboards. | **Test**: A crafted sequence of intersectional filter queries across department, role, and location cannot isolate or identify an individual employee. | `FEATURE_PRIVACY_ANALYTICS` |
| **14** | **Leadership Briefing Generator** | Phase 4 | Auto-drafts an editable monthly briefing for the CLO summarizing skill movements, ROI, and missing data, with every claim linked to underlying evidence. | **Test**: A leadership briefing cannot be exported or distributed without explicit CLO human approval, reason logging, and an audit trail entry. | `FEATURE_LEADERSHIP_BRIEFING`|
